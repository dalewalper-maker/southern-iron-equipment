Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I view and pick up equipment?

Equipment is staged at our Lethbridge yard, 1065 - 36th Street North, Lethbridge, AB T1H 6L5. Call or text 780-901-1573 to confirm a unit is on the yard before you drive out — units are regularly moved between southern Alberta locations.

What kind of heavy equipment do you list?

We list used and new forklifts, telehandlers, excavators, skid steers, backhoe loaders, compact track loaders, teletrucks, aerial work platforms, and other material handling and construction equipment. Brands available include JCB, Tailift, AUSA, Starke, LMH, Hyundai, Hyster, Toyota, Caterpillar, John Deere, Merlo and Yale.

Why are prices not shown on the website?

Pricing varies by configuration, location, financing terms and trade value. We provide direct, current pricing fast — text or call 780-901-1573 with the unit number, or email for a written quote.

Do you deliver to Medicine Hat, Cranbrook, Fernie and the Kootenays?

Yes. We regularly transport equipment from Lethbridge to Medicine Hat and across southern Alberta, plus southeast British Columbia including Fernie, Cranbrook, Sparwood, Nelson and the wider Kootenay region. Transport is quoted per unit and route.

Do you offer financing on used and new heavy equipment?

Yes — dealer financing programs are available for qualifying buyers, including JCB dealer financing. Ask for current rates and terms when requesting your quote.

How current is the equipment list on this site?

Inventory updates from the master dealer price list. Status is shown on every unit: In Stock, Incoming, or Check Availability. If a unit is listed, it is current as of the latest update.

Do you take trades?

Yes — we take trades on most units. Send year, make, model, hours and a few photos when requesting your quote and we'll value your trade as part of the deal.
