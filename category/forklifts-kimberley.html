Serving Kimberley, BC

Used & New Forklifts in Kimberley, BC

Up the hill from Cranbrook in the Purcell Mountains, Kimberley is a former mining town turned year-round resort community. Local industry runs on tourism and resort operations and construction and residential development, and that's exactly where forklifts earn their keep. Our forklifts units around here see indoor distribution and dock work. From our Lethbridge yard we deliver into the broader corridor — Cranbrook, Marysville, Wasa and Ta Ta Creek, alongside mountain town in the east kootenays. We deliver into Kimberley on our regular East Kootenay runs through Cranbrook. 53 forklifts units currently in stock and ready to move from Lethbridge to Kimberley on request.
