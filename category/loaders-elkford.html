Used & New Wheel Loaders in Elkford, BC

The highest town in BC, Elkford sits at the head of the Elk Valley surrounded by Teck's coal operations and Rocky Mountain wilderness. Local industry runs on forestry and outdoor recreation, and that's exactly where loaders earn their keep. Our loaders units around here see ag operations (bale and feed handling) and yard duty (gravel, snow, manure). From our Lethbridge yard we deliver into the broader corridor — Sparwood, Fernie and the Elk Valley coal complexes, alongside the top of the elk valley.
